## 1. Project Vision

AgriPulse is an AI-powered agricultural market intelligence and decision-support platform.

The objective is to develop a complete web application that helps farmers, agricultural traders, wholesalers, retailers, food-processing companies, institutional buyers, and other agricultural purchasers make informed decisions using agricultural market data and machine learning.

This is NOT intended to be a simple crop-price prediction application.

The platform should combine:
- Historical agricultural market prices.
- Agricultural demand and supply analysis.
- Weather and climate influences on agricultural prices.
- Machine learning-based price forecasting.
- Agricultural market intelligence.
- Data-driven decision support.
- Interactive dashboards and visualizations.

Additional useful features can be introduced as the architecture develops.

## 2. Main Objectives

1. Collect and process agricultural market datasets.
2. Analyze historical agricultural price movements.
3. Investigate relationships between demand, supply, weather, and prices.
4. Develop and evaluate suitable ML forecasting models.
5. Present agricultural market intelligence through interactive visualizations.
6. Provide useful decision-support insights.
7. Build a complete, responsive, professional web application.

## 3. Intended Users

- Farmers
- Agricultural traders
- Wholesalers
- Retailers
- Food-processing companies
- Institutional agricultural buyers
- Other agricultural market participants
